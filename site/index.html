Two minutes before the call

One click, three answers.

Which camera, which mic, and which speakers your Mac will use, and whether each one works. No more “can you hear me?” for the first minute.

  1. 1

    Click the camera icon

    It sits next to the clock. The first time, macOS asks for camera and mic access. Live previews run while the panel is open. Optional microphone startup checks briefly read audio levels when another app opens a mic.

  2. 2

    Check all three

    See yourself in the preview. Talk and watch the meter move. Click Test to hear a tone in your left ear, then your right.

  3. 3

    Save it as your setup

    Picking a mic or speaker makes it your Mac's default, the same as System Settings. Then click Save as my setup, and Green Room remembers it.

During the call

See what your call is really using.

Zoom and Meet keep their own device choices, so your Mac can look right while the call uses the wrong mic. Green Room shows which app has each mic, speaker, and camera open, and tells you when it isn't your setup.

AirPods have a catch: the moment an app turns on their mic, your voice and everything you hear drop to phone quality. Green Room spots it and offers your Mac's own mic instead.

Every warning comes with the fix, one click away in the panel or right in the alert.

Before you go on

It speaks up at the right moment.

No need to remember to check. Green Room looks ahead to your calls, keeps your devices where you left them, and lets you hear exactly what others will.

Checks before your calls

Two minutes before a calendar event with a Zoom, Meet, or Teams link, it checks your setup. Ready means silence. Not ready means one alert with the fix.

Switches back when AirPods take over

AirPods out of the case or an iPhone nearby grabs your sound? Green Room puts your setup back and says so. Meant it? Keep them with one click.

Lets you hear yourself

Record five seconds and hear them played back, so you know how you sound, not just that the mic works. Nothing is saved.

Setups for every place you work. Save Desk, Studio, and Travel; Green Room picks the one whose devices are plugged in. Right-click the icon to switch setups or devices without opening the panel, or press ⌃⌥⌘G to open it from anywhere.

Which mic is hearing you? Click Compare for a live level on every mic at once, then click the one that moves. Rename “USB Audio Device” to “Lapel mic” in Settings, and hide the virtual devices you never use.

When a device goes missing

Not just missing. Missing because.

Green Room keeps a history of every camera, mic, and speaker you use. When one that's usually here disappears, it tells you why, and what to try first.

USB

Not plugged in

Your Mac doesn't see it on USB at all.

Try the cable, the hub, or another port.

USB

Plugged in, but not working

It's on USB, but not showing up as a camera or mic.

Try unplugging it and plugging it back in.

Bluetooth

Not connected

It's off, or not paired right now.

Try turning it on, or connect it in Bluetooth settings.

Works from day one. Green Room also reads macOS's own memory of USB audio devices, so a mic you've used before shows up before Green Room has any history of its own.

Connected isn't the same as working

Catch the silent mic.

A mic can be plugged in, picked, and still send nothing. Under the meter, Green Room tells you when macOS has it muted, when its input volume is at zero, or when no sound has come in for five seconds.

That last one is usually the mute button or gain knob on the mic itself, the one you bumped while packing up yesterday.

Before you hit record

Ready to record, not just ready to talk.

A call forgives a 720p camera and a laptop on battery. A recording doesn't. Open Ready to record? in the panel for the things that ruin a take: the camera's resolution and frame rate, the mic's quality, free disk space, and power.

Start recording in Loom, OBS, Screen Studio, QuickTime, or Riverside and Green Room runs this check on its own, then speaks up only if something would spoil the take.

When the camera is below 1080p, it tells you why: an app picked a smaller size, or the camera or capture card can't do better right now. The fixes are different.

Under the preview, one line about light and framing: a covered lens, a dark room, a backlit face, or sitting off-center. It looks at about one frame a second, on your Mac, and keeps nothing.

Questions

Does Green Room record me?

Live previews run while the panel or Record Preview is open. Optional microphone startup checks briefly read audio levels when another app opens a mic. Hear Yourself holds a short recording only in memory for playback. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Will it change my camera in Zoom or Google Meet?

Picking a mic or speaker in Green Room makes it your Mac's default, the same as System Settings › Sound, so most apps follow it. Picking a camera sets the one macOS suggests to apps. Zoom and Meet keep their own camera setting, so pick it there once too.

How does it know a device is usually here?

Green Room keeps a history of every camera, mic, and speaker it sees. A device that's been connected on most recent days counts as usual. For USB audio devices it also reads macOS's own memory, so a mic you've used before shows up from the first day.

My mic is plugged in. Why does it say Not plugged in?

It means your Mac doesn't see the mic on USB at all, even though the plug is in. That points to the cable, a hub, or the port. Try another port, or plug it straight into your Mac.

What happens when I unplug my dock?

You get one alert that lists every usual device that left with it, not a pile of alerts. Turn alerts off any time in Settings.

Can I see which mic is picking me up?

Yes. Click Compare next to the microphone picker and every mic gets its own live level. Talk, and the one hearing you moves; click it to switch. AirPods and iPhone mics say Switch to test instead, because opening their mic drops AirPods to call quality or wakes your phone.

Does it work with AirPods and other Bluetooth devices?

Yes. When an app turns on a headset's mic, it drops into call mode and everything sounds like a phone call; Green Room tells you and offers a better mic. When a usual Bluetooth device is missing, it says Not connected and tells you to turn it on or connect it in Bluetooth settings.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, and it updates itself.

Does Green Room read my calendar?

Only if you enable Check before calendar calls or Include calendar calls under Hold calls in Settings. It reads event times and video-call links, never changes your calendar, and never sends it anywhere.

Yes. The source is on GitHub under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-MIT), and each version becomes MIT two years after it's released.
