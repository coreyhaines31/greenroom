Mac guide · macOS 14 Sonoma and later

Logitech webcam not working on Mac? Fix it in order.

The C920, C922, Brio, StreamCam, and MX Brio all use the camera driver built into macOS, so there's nothing to install. When one won't show up, it's almost always the port, the hub, the cable, or a permission.

Quick check first: open the free webcam test and pick your Logitech camera. If you see yourself there, the camera works and the problem is in one app (steps 4 and 5). If it isn't in the list, start at step 1.

Fix a Logitech webcam, step by step

  1. Plug it straight into your Mac.Logitech says to skip the hub or dock, since some hubs can't supply enough power for a webcam. Try another port. If it's on a USB 3 port, Logitech also suggests trying a USB 2 port.
  2. Check the adapter on a Brio.Logitech says some USB-C to USB-A adapters and hubs aren't fully compatible with the Brio on a Mac, and that you may need a USB-C to USB-C cable instead. For 4K, the Brio needs a USB 3 connection.
  3. See whether your Mac detects it.Hold Option, choose Apple menu › System Information, and click Camera. Logitech says a Brio shows up there by name, like Logitech BRIO, or as Logitech Camera on older macOS. If it isn't listed, the problem is the cable, the port, or the camera. Logitech says that if a second computer can't see it either, the camera may be faulty.
  4. Give the app permission.Choose Apple menu › System Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera, and turn on the app you're using. Apple's own apps, like FaceTime and Photo Booth, don't need this, so Photo Booth is a quick way to see whether the camera works at all.
  5. Pick it in the app.Zoom, Meet, Teams, and OBS keep their own camera choice, and may still be set to your MacBook's camera or your iPhone through Continuity Camera. Open the app's video settings and choose the Logitech camera.
  6. Quit other video apps, then restart.An app that still has the camera open can keep it from starting somewhere else. Quit it, reopen the app you need, and restart your Mac if the camera still won't start.

Do you need Logitech's software?

Not to make the camera work. Logitech says it doesn't make Mac drivers for these cameras, and that features like RightLight and RightSound are built into the webcam's firmware. You only need an app to change settings like zoom, field of view, HDR, or anti-flicker.

  • Logi Options+ is what Logitech recommends for most of its webcams, including the MX Brio. It needs macOS 14 or later.
  • Logi Tune is Logitech's app for its business webcams, like the Brio 500.
  • G HUB is Logitech's app for gaming and streaming.

Logitech's older Camera Settings app has reached end of life, and Logitech points to Logi Tune instead. If your settings don't stick, Logitech says to keep Options+ running in the background and open it before Zoom or OBS. If Options+ or G HUB stopped opening on your Mac, Logitech released a patch on January 7, 2026, and says to install it rather than uninstall the app.

Picture flickers, or looks dark?

  • Flicker or rolling bands. Set Anti-flicker to match your local power: 60 Hz in the US, 50 Hz in most of Europe. Logitech puts it in Options+ or G HUB for the Brio and MX Brio, and in Logi Tune for the Brio 500.
  • Dark or choppy in a dim room. Logitech's RightLight lowers the frame rate to let in more light, so a dim room looks choppy. Logitech also suggests turning HDR off in low light on the MX Brio. A lamp in front of you fixes both.
  • Stuck below 4K. 4K needs a USB 3 connection. A USB 2 port, an older hub, or the wrong adapter keeps you at a lower resolution.

The webcam test shows the resolution and frame rate you're really getting, and flags a dark or backlit picture.

Webcam mic not working, or picking you up instead of your good mic?

Most Logitech webcams have their own mic, and it shows up in your Mac's Input list as a separate device. If people can't hear you through it, select it in System Settings › Sound › Input and raise the Input volume, which is Logitech's own fix. If you have a better mic, check that your call app isn't using the webcam's. See Mac microphone not working for the rest.

What Green Room does automatically

Green Room is a free menu bar app that keeps an eye on your camera, mic, and speakers, so you aren't troubleshooting while people wait.

  • Says why a webcam is missing. When your Logitech camera disappears, it tells you whether your Mac sees it on USB at all, or sees it but can't use it, so you know whether to check the cable or the app.
  • Shows which mic is hearing you. Compare mics puts a live level on every mic at once, so you can tell whether the call is getting the webcam's mic or the one you meant to use.
  • Checks before you record. It reports the camera's resolution and frame rate, and flags a covered lens, a dark room, or a backlit face.
