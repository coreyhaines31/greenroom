Comparison · Mac audio apps

Looking for a SoundSource alternative? Check what you need it for.

SoundSource is the best way to set volume, output, and effects for each app on a Mac. Green Room does a different job: it makes sure your camera, mic, and speakers are right before a call, and keeps your Mac on the ones you choose. Many people want one, some want both.

The short version: want Spotify quieter than Zoom, or an EQ on your headphones? That's SoundSource. Tired of AirPods taking your mic, the wrong camera on a call, or finding out you were muted? That's Green Room, and it's free.

Choose SoundSource if you want

  • A volume for every app. Turn down a game without touching your call, or mute one noisy app.
  • Apps on different outputs. Music on your speakers while the call stays in your headphones.
  • Better sound. A 10-band EQ, Audio Unit plugins, Magic Boost, and Headphone EQ, per app or for everything.

It's $49 from Rogue Amoeba, for macOS 15 Sequoia and later. The trial has every feature, so you can try it properly before you buy.

Choose Green Room if you want

  • Your Mac on the right devices. Rank your mics, speakers, and cameras, and Green Room switches to the highest one that's plugged in, so AirPods or your iPhone never take over. See how to stop AirPods switching devices.
  • A check before every call. A live camera preview, a mic meter, Hear Yourself, and a speaker test in one panel, with a warning when Zoom or Meet is using a different mic than yours.
  • Your camera too. A full-screen record preview with grid lines and crop guides, and a recording check when Loom, OBS, or QuickTime starts.

It's free, source available, and needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Using both

They don't overlap much, so they run fine together. Green Room decides which mic, speakers, and camera your Mac uses. SoundSource decides how loud each app is on them, and what it sounds like. If you turn on Green Room's Priority list, let it pick the devices, so the two apps aren't switching the same thing.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to SoundSource?

For per-app volume and EQ, Green Room isn't one: it doesn't change any app's volume. If what you want from SoundSource is switching your mic and speakers from the menu bar, and keeping your Mac on the right ones, Green Room does that for free.

How much does SoundSource cost?

Rogue Amoeba lists SoundSource at $49, and $25 to upgrade from SoundSource 4 or 5. The free trial has every feature, but adds noise to the audio it adjusts after 20 minutes until you relaunch it.

Can I use SoundSource and Green Room together?

Yes. They do different jobs. Green Room picks which mic, speakers, and camera your Mac uses, and SoundSource sets each app's volume, output, and effects.

Does Green Room change volume for each app?

No. Green Room shows which app is using each mic and speaker, and switches your Mac's devices. It doesn't change any app's volume or add effects.

Which macOS versions do they need?

SoundSource 6 needs macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Green Room needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

SoundSource is a product of Rogue Amoeba Software. Green Room isn't affiliated with Rogue Amoeba.
